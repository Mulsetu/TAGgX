## Posting checklist (viral basics)

1. Hook in **first 1–2 seconds** (pain or curiosity).  
2. On-screen captions always (sound-off).  
3. One idea per video — don’t explain the whole platform.  
4. End with **logo + URL + one CTA**.  
5. Post 3–5 variants of Script 1 with different hooks.  
6. Reply to comments with demo link within the hour.  
7. Pin comment: `Demo → tagx.mulsetu.com/demo`
